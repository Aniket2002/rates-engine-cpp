# v2.2 SOFR verification record

The extension is checked at three levels: C++ lifecycle invariants, independent
QuantLib contracts, and an offline replay of captured public fixings.

## Expanded lifecycle and two-curve quote risk (22 October 2026)

The [expanded report](results/sofr/expanded/report.json),
[scenario summary](results/sofr/expanded/case-summary.csv) and
[quote-risk comparisons](results/sofr/expanded/quote-risk-comparisons.csv) record
**30,524/30,524** independent comparisons with QuantLib 1.44. Full daily comparison
rows are regenerated by `validate_sofr.py`. The
[expanded captured-fixing replay](results/sofr/expanded/replay-report.json) passes
**40/40** comparisons and still rejects all four invalid-input scenarios.
Release CTest passes **87/87** after correcting and rerunning the new C++ test's
curve-coverage fixture; the other 86 tests passed in the full run.
Changed C++ files pass the formatting check. The
[extension evidence](results/sofr/expanded/verification.json) records source hashes.

Five schedule families cover regular quarterly periods, leap-day/end-of-month
starts, year-end and holiday maturities, two-month intervals and short final
stubs. They cross lookback, shift and lockout with future, seasoned, weekend,
matured/unpaid and settled states. Calendar checks include government-bond and
weekend-only index calendars plus distinct payment holidays; payment lags range
from zero to four days. Fixed accrual includes ACT/360, ACT/365F and 30E/360,
with following and modified-following adjustment. Synthetic fixtures use explicit
next-calendar-day availability for weekend accrued checks, not NY Fed publication
timing. The captured replay retains first-local-capture availability.

The two-curve quote-risk reference builds fresh QuantLib OIS helper curves for
every shock. Discount quote shocks rebuild **both** curves while keeping
projection quotes fixed; projection shocks keep discounting fixed. Checks cover
parallel and pillar sensitivities, different discount/projection coupon frequencies,
unsorted inputs, negative rates, receiver trades, observation shift/lockout,
short stubs, payment-date inclusion and 0.5/2 bp bumps across the lifecycle.
The captured payer replay independently checks 1 bp buckets and parallel shocks.
Risk tolerance is `2e-5` currency/bp; dates match exactly. All realized fixings
remain unchanged. These scenarios use **synthetic OIS quotes** and do not validate
basis-swap calibration, clearing or collateral processing.

## Independent models

[Lifecycle report](results/sofr/lifecycle/report.json) and
[comparison rows](results/sofr/lifecycle/comparisons.csv) cover future, seasoned,
matured/unpaid and settled states, payment-date inclusion/exclusion, two-day
lookback with/without observation shift, two-day lockout, explicit government
bond holidays, daily fixing dates/weights and spot/payment-lag calibration.

QuantLib independently generates schedules, coupons and OIS helper curves.
No C++ curve factors are inputs to its bootstrap. Cash-flow/PV tolerance is
`2e-7` currency on 1m notional; clean PV uses `3e-7`, curve factors `5e-10`,
and input repricing `1e-12` decimal rate. Dates and counts require exact matches.
Accrued is independently computed using this project's documented prorated
observation convention; it is not asserted equal to QuantLib's shifted accrued.

[Captured-fixing replay](results/sofr/replay/report.json) checks actual NY Fed
observations with **synthetic OIS quotes**. QuantLib independently calibrates
single and separate projection curves, prices the seasoned swap, and repeats
discount/projection zero-node shocks. Tolerance is `2e-5` currency for cash flows
and PV, and `2e-5` currency/bp for risk. Missing history, corrupt snapshots,
unavailable capture and stale quotes are rejected. The
[full example output](results/sofr/example) includes cash flows, observations,
calibration, risk and input provenance.

The suite caught a real normalization defect: a shifted observation period
can have a different number of calendar days from its contractual interest
period. Normalizing the compound rate before applying contractual accrual
corrected both coupon amounts and calibration; tolerances were not relaxed.

**98.48% lines** and **86.12% branches** across library source/headers. It excludes
Python, bindings, command-line programs and dependencies. LLVM emitted the
same 32 function-profile mismatch warnings seen in earlier local reports;
the raw diagnostics are retained. Coverage is useful path evidence, not proof
of mathematical completeness. Remaining branches include validation failures
